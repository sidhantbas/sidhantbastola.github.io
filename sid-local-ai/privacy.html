Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

sid-local-ai is a personal tool used only by its owner, Sidhant Bastola, with his own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads and manages the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks through Google's APIs.

Where the data goes

  • Data from Google is processed only on the owner's own computer, by language models running locally.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • It is not sent to any cloud AI service and is not used to train AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
